Slingshot Aerospace was awarded a contract by the U.S. Space Force’s Space Systems Command to further develop its already operational GPS jamming detection technology by incorporating enhanced geolocation and artificial intelligence (AI) to detect threats around the globe in near real-time and help foreshadow future threats to international security. The technology detects ground-based GPS jamming and spoofing, which can be used by adversaries to reduce combat effectiveness and wreak havoc on commercial industry.
